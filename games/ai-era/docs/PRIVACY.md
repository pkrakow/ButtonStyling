# Privacy notice

Draft for review. Before release, the publisher must fill in the bracketed items and host this notice at a public URL.

## Summary

Runtime: Takeoff can share **anonymous gameplay data** with its developers. Sharing is **off** unless you turn it on, either on the first-launch screen or in Settings. You can turn it off at any time.

## What is collected when sharing is on

- **Gameplay events:** runs started and ended, each decision (the options you were shown, the one you picked, and how long you took), pauses, quits, map view changes, button presses, screens viewed, and settings changes.
- **Context:** game version, build type, operating system name, language code, screen size, and, on Steam, the build ID and whether you are on a Steam Deck.
- **Identifiers:** a random install ID created on your device, a random ID for each session, and a random ID for each run. None of these come from your account or hardware.

## What is never collected

Your Steam ID, Steam name, or any account details. Your name, email, or contacts. Anything you type. Seeds are numbers derived from what you enter, never the text itself. The game itself does not collect your IP address. [Publisher: confirm that the ingest server drops or truncates IP addresses.]

## Why

To learn which choices players find confusing, slow, or unbalanced, and to improve the game. Legal basis: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a), ePrivacy Art. 5(3)).

## Where it goes

Events are written to a file on your device, then uploaded in batches to [Publisher: analytics provider and region]. If you are offline, they wait in a local queue and upload later. Batches the server keeps rejecting are deleted after five attempts.

[Publisher: retention period, 12 months or less.]

## Your choices and rights

- **Turn sharing off:** go to Settings and switch off "Share anonymous play data". This stops recording and deletes all play data stored on this device that has not been uploaded.
- **Reset your ID:** in Settings, "Reset ID" creates a new random ID and deletes data stored on this device under the old one.
- **Access or delete uploaded data:** email [Publisher: contact email] with the install ID shown in Settings. You can also object or complain to your data protection authority.

## Local files

Settings, the local event log, and an error log live in the game's user data folder. Use Settings > "Open data folder" to see them. The error log records technical failures only, such as an upload that failed, and stays on your device.

## Steam

Achievements and Steam stats are handled by Valve under the Steam Privacy Agreement. Steam only sees aggregated totals of these, such as how many runs reached each ending.
